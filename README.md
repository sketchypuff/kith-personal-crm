# Kith

A fully on-device personal CRM for iPhone. Kith reminds you to stay in touch with
the people who matter, on a cadence you choose per person.

- **Set a cadence per person.** Pick someone from your contacts, choose how often you want to reach out, and pick the day and time you want to be nudged.
- **Work a daily list.** Today shows who is due, ranked, and you check them off, push them to tomorrow, or skip.
- **Keep the context.** Each person carries key dates, tags, notes, and a timeline of every past touch.

Everything stays on the device. No backend, no accounts, no analytics, with
optional iCloud sync through a private CloudKit database.
